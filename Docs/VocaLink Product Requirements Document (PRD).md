# **VocaLink Product Requirements Document (PRD)**

## **1\. Product Overview**

**Product Name:** VocaLink

**Product Type:** Mobile-first vocational skills and employment marketplace

**Primary Market:** Nigeria

**Core Promise:**  
**Skill → Training → Certification → Work**

VocaLink is a trusted platform that helps young Nigerians discover verified vocational training, connect with qualified trainers and training centres, pursue recognised skills certification, and transition into internships, mentorship and employment.

The platform also helps training providers reach learners, businesses find skilled workers, and organisations manage skills-development programmes.

VocaLink is designed to address three major gaps:

1. Difficulty finding legitimate vocational training opportunities.  
2. Limited awareness and access to recognised skills certification.  
3. Weak connections between training and actual employment opportunities.

---

# **2\. Product Vision**

To become Nigeria’s trusted digital gateway for acquiring practical skills, getting certified and connecting skilled people with opportunities to work.

---

# **3\. Target Users**

## **Primary Users: Learners**

Nigerians aged approximately 16–35, including:

* School leavers  
* Graduates  
* Unemployed young people  
* Career changers  
* Existing artisans seeking additional skills  
* People seeking affordable or free vocational training  
* People seeking certification for skills they already possess

## **Secondary Users**

### **Training Providers**

* Vocational training centres  
* Technical colleges and institutes  
* Certified trainers  
* Master artisans  
* Private training organisations

### **Employers**

* SMEs  
* Workshops  
* Companies  
* Construction businesses  
* Fashion businesses  
* Hospitality businesses  
* Technology and creative businesses

### **Programme Partners**

* NGOs  
* Government agencies  
* Development organisations  
* Corporate social responsibility programmes  
* Skills-development initiatives

### **Administrators**

VocaLink administrators responsible for managing the marketplace, verification, users, programmes, certification pathways, opportunities and platform settings.

---

# **4\. Product Goals**

VocaLink should:

* Make legitimate vocational training easier to discover.  
* Reduce exposure to fraudulent or misleading training providers.  
* Help learners compare training opportunities before enrolling.  
* Connect learners with appropriate training based on their interests and circumstances.  
* Make certification pathways easier to understand and access.  
* Create a clear transition from training to work.  
* Give businesses access to a pool of skilled and certified talent.  
* Help organisations reach and manage beneficiaries.  
* Provide administrators with control over the marketplace and major product features.

---

# **5\. Core User Journey**

The primary learner journey should be:

**Discover → Explore → Choose → Enrol → Learn → Complete → Certify → Prepare for Work → Connect → Work**

A learner should be able to enter VocaLink without knowing exactly what skill or training they want and still receive useful guidance.

---

# **6\. Core Features**

## **6.1 Learner Registration and Profile**

Learners should be able to create a personal profile containing information such as:

* Name  
* Age range  
* Location  
* Education level  
* Existing skills  
* Skills they want to learn  
* Career interests  
* Preferred learning format  
* Training budget  
* Availability

The profile should become the basis for relevant training and opportunity recommendations.

### **Recommendation**

Use a simple onboarding process rather than asking for too much information at registration. Additional information can be collected progressively as the learner uses the platform.

---

# **7\. Training Discovery**

VocaLink should provide two discovery methods.

## **Search and Browse**

Learners can search for training using:

* Trade or skill  
* Location  
* Training provider  
* Keyword

They should be able to filter results by:

* Free or paid  
* Online or physical  
* Location  
* Duration  
* Certification availability  
* Start date  
* Provider verification status  
* Beginner/intermediate/advanced level

## **Guided Discovery**

Learners who do not know what to learn can answer a short series of questions covering:

* Interests  
* Career goals  
* Existing experience  
* Location  
* Preferred learning method  
* Budget  
* Availability

VocaLink then recommends relevant skills and training programmes.

### **Admin Control**

Guided Discovery should be controlled from the admin portal.

Administrators should be able to:

* Turn Guided Discovery on or off.  
* Enable or disable specific questions.  
* Manage recommendation categories.  
* Control which training programmes can appear in recommendations.

---

# **8\. Training Programme Listings**

Every programme should have a detailed listing containing:

* Programme title  
* Skill/trade category  
* Description  
* Training provider  
* Provider verification status  
* Training location  
* Online/offline format  
* Duration  
* Start date  
* Cost  
* Payment information  
* Certification information  
* Entry requirements  
* What learners will learn  
* Available spaces  
* Application/enrolment process  
* Contact/support information  
* Reviews and ratings

A learner should be able to understand the programme without needing to contact the provider first.

---

# **9\. Training Provider Verification**

Trust should be a major differentiator of VocaLink.

Every provider should go through a verification process before being allowed to present themselves as verified.

## **Verification Process**

Providers submit relevant information and supporting evidence.

VocaLink administrators review the submission and either:

* Approve the provider  
* Request additional information  
* Reject the application

Approved providers receive a visible **Verified Provider** badge.

### **Important distinction**

Verification should communicate that VocaLink has reviewed the provider. It should not automatically imply that every programme offered by that provider is officially accredited.

Where applicable, programmes should separately identify recognised accreditation or certification pathways.

---

# **10\. Learner Ratings, Reviews and Reporting**

After participating in training, learners should be able to:

* Rate their experience.  
* Write a review.  
* Report misleading information.  
* Report poor service.  
* Report suspected fraud.  
* Confirm whether the programme delivered what was advertised.

Reviews should appear on provider and programme profiles.

Administrators should be able to review complaints and moderate inappropriate or fraudulent reviews.

### **Recommendation**

Do not make ratings the only measure of trust. Display verification status, number of reviews, programme history and relevant certification information alongside ratings.

---

# **11\. Enrolment**

Learners should be able to select a programme and begin the enrolment process from VocaLink.

Depending on the programme, enrolment may involve:

* Direct registration  
* Application  
* Provider approval  
* Payment  
* Scholarship selection  
* Eligibility verification

Learners should receive confirmation and have a record of the training programmes they have enrolled in.

---

# **12\. Free Training and Paid Training**

VocaLink should support both:

### **Free Opportunities**

Examples include:

* Government programmes  
* NGO-funded training  
* Development-partner programmes  
* Sponsored training  
* Community programmes

### **Paid Opportunities**

Learners should be able to see:

* Total programme cost  
* Payment terms  
* What is included  
* Additional expected costs

### **Recommendation**

Clearly label **Free**, **Sponsored**, and **Paid** programmes so users can immediately understand the financial commitment.

---

# **13\. Learning Experience**

VocaLink should support both:

**Online learning** and **Offline/physical learning**.

For online programmes, learners should be able to access learning materials and track their progress.

For physical programmes, VocaLink should provide information about:

* Training location  
* Schedule  
* Trainer  
* Attendance expectations  
* Programme duration

The product should not assume that every vocational skill can be taught entirely online.

---

# **14\. Learner Progress**

Learners should have a dashboard showing:

* Enrolled programmes  
* Current learning status  
* Completed programmes  
* Upcoming sessions  
* Certificates  
* Certification pathways  
* Internship opportunities  
* Job opportunities

A simple progress indicator should help learners understand where they are in their skills journey.

---

# **15\. Skills Certification**

Certification should be a core part of VocaLink rather than an optional extra.

The platform should help learners understand:

* What certification is relevant to their skill.  
* Whether their training supports a certification pathway.  
* What they need to do to qualify.  
* Where and how assessment takes place.  
* The status of their certification journey.

Where applicable, VocaLink should facilitate access to **NSQ-related certification pathways** through appropriate recognised bodies and providers.

### **Recommendation**

Do not promise certification merely because a learner completes a VocaLink-listed training programme. Clearly separate:

**Training Completion → Assessment → Certification**

This protects trust and prevents misleading claims.

---

# **16\. Skills Passport**

Each learner should have a digital skills profile that can eventually serve as a **VocaLink Skills Passport**.

It may contain:

* Skills acquired  
* Completed training  
* Training providers  
* Certificates  
* Certification status  
* Experience  
* Portfolio  
* Reviews or endorsements  
* Internship history

The learner should be able to use this profile when applying for opportunities.

# **20\. Employer Experience**

Employers should be able to:

* Create a business profile.  
* Post vacancies.  
* Search for skilled candidates.  
* View candidate profiles.  
* Review skills and certificates.  
* Review portfolios.  
* Contact candidates.  
* Manage applications.

### **Recommendation**

Give employers the ability to search by **skill, location, experience and certification**, rather than relying only on job applications.

* Create a provider profile.  
* Submit verification information.

* Set programme costs.  
* Specify locations.  
* Identify certification pathways.  
* Manage enrolments.  
* Communicate with learners.  
* View learner feedback.  
* Monitor programme performance.

Learners should be able to discover opportunities by:

* State  
* City  
* Local area

# **26\. Messaging**

VocaLink should provide a controlled communication channel between:

* Learners and providers  
* Learners and employers  
* Learners and mentors

Messaging should help users ask questions before enrolling or applying.

### **Recommendation**

Keep communication inside VocaLink rather than immediately redirecting users to WhatsApp. This gives the platform better control over trust, records, complaints and user protection.

* Organisations

### **Marketplace**

* Skills  
* Categories  
* Training programmes  
* Jobs

### **Trust and Safety**

* Complaints

* Certification information

# **29\. Trust and Safety Principles**

Trust should be built into every stage of the product.

VocaLink should:

* Verify providers.  
* Clearly distinguish verified providers from unverified applicants.  
* Encourage learner reviews.  
* Provide reporting mechanisms.  
* Investigate complaints.  
* Flag misleading listings.  
* Clearly communicate programme costs.  
* Clearly distinguish training completion from official certification.  
* Avoid allowing misleading employment or certification claims.

### **Provider Revenue**

Training providers may pay for:

* Premium visibility  
* Featured programmes  
* Promotional placements  
* Business tools

### **Employer Revenue**

Businesses may pay for:

* Premium job listings  
* Candidate search  
* Recruitment services

### **Institutional Revenue**

NGOs, government agencies and development partners may pay for:

* Programme management  
* Beneficiary management  
* Reporting  
* Recruitment of beneficiaries  
* Skills programme promotion

### **Certification and Services**

VocaLink may generate revenue through eligible services connected to training and certification while keeping the learner experience transparent.

Avoid making access to legitimate training dependent on payment. VocaLink's trust proposition will be stronger if valuable free opportunities remain discoverable alongside commercial programmes.

# **34\. Product Principles**

VocaLink should be built around five principles:

### **Trust First**

Users should be able to distinguish legitimate providers and opportunities from questionable ones.

### **Access**

Users should be able to discover opportunities regardless of income level.

### **Outcomes Over Activity**

Success should be measured by skills acquired, certification and employment—not merely registrations.

### **Simplicity**

A young person should be able to find a suitable training opportunity without needing specialist knowledge.

### **Opportunity After Training**

VocaLink should not stop at education. The platform should help users turn skills into economic opportunities.

# **35\. Example Learner Journey**

**David, 22, has just completed secondary school and wants to learn a practical skill.**

He downloads VocaLink and selects his location and interests.

Guided Discovery recommends several trades, including electrical installation, welding and photography.

David chooses electrical installation.

He sees several programmes and compares:

* Provider verification  
* Location  
* Cost  
* Duration  
* Learning format  
* Certification pathway  
* Reviews

He selects a verified training provider and enrols.

During training, David tracks his progress through VocaLink.

After completion, VocaLink shows him the next steps towards the relevant certification pathway.

David completes his certification process and updates his VocaLink Skills Passport.

He then searches for internships and jobs.

An electrical services company finds David through his skills profile and portfolio.

David applies, is selected for an opportunity, and begins his transition from **skill acquisition to employment**.

# **36\. VocaLink Positioning**

VocaLink should not position itself simply as another online learning platform.

Its core differentiation should be:

**A trusted pathway from vocational skill discovery to training, certification and work.**

The value proposition for each major user is:

**Learner:**  
“Find the right skill, learn from a trusted provider, get certified and find opportunities to work.”

**Training Provider:**  
“Reach learners who are actively looking for practical skills training.”

**Employer:**  
“Find skilled people with verified training, certification and practical portfolios.”

**NGO/Government:**  
“Connect beneficiaries with skills programmes and track their journey towards employment.”

---

# **37\. Product Success Statement**

VocaLink is successful when a Nigerian who wants to become economically productive can enter the platform with either **a skill they want to learn or simply a desire to earn**, discover a trustworthy pathway, complete relevant training, pursue recognised certification and find a genuine opportunity to apply that skill.

**VocaLink: From Skill to Opportunity.**
