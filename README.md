# VocaLink — From Skill to Opportunity

**VocaLink** is a mobile-first vocational skills and employment marketplace for Nigeria.

**Core Promise:** Skill → Training → Certification → Work

VocaLink helps young Nigerians discover verified vocational training, connect with qualified trainers and training centres, pursue recognised skills certification, and transition into internships, mentorship and employment.

## Vision

To become Nigeria's trusted digital gateway for acquiring practical skills, getting certified and connecting skilled people with opportunities to work.

## Problem We Solve

1. Difficulty finding legitimate vocational training opportunities
2. Limited awareness and access to recognised skills certification
3. Weak connections between training and actual employment opportunities

## Who It's For

**Primary — Learners (16–35):**
School leavers, graduates, unemployed youth, career changers, existing artisans, seekers of affordable/free training or certification for existing skills.

**Secondary:**
- **Training Providers:** vocational centres, technical colleges, certified trainers, master artisans, private training organisations
- **Employers:** SMEs, workshops, construction, fashion, hospitality, tech and creative businesses
- **Programme Partners:** NGOs, government agencies, development organisations, CSR programmes
- **Administrators:** VocaLink marketplace operators

## Core User Journey

Discover → Explore → Choose → Enrol → Learn → Complete → Certify → Prepare for Work → Connect → Work

A learner can enter without knowing what skill they want and still get useful guidance via Guided Discovery.

## Key Features

- **Learner profiles & recommendations** — progressive onboarding based on interests, location, budget, availability
- **Training discovery** — search/browse by trade, location, provider + filters (free/paid, online/physical, duration, certification, verification, level) + Guided Discovery Q&A
- **Programme listings** — detailed: provider, verification status, cost, duration, format, certification, entry requirements, curriculum, reviews
- **Provider verification** — admin review → Verified Provider badge. Verification ≠ automatic accreditation
- **Ratings, reviews & reporting** — learner feedback, fraud / misleading-info reports, admin moderation
- **Enrolment** — direct registration, application, approval, payment / scholarship / eligibility flows
- **Free & paid training** — clearly labelled Free / Sponsored / Paid, including government & NGO programmes
- **Learning support** — online materials + progress tracking; offline location / schedule / trainer info
- **Learner dashboard** — enrolled programmes, progress, certificates, certification pathways, opportunities
- **Certification pathways** — what certification is relevant, requirements, assessment location, status. Including NSQ-related pathways. Training Completion → Assessment → Certification are kept distinct
- **Skills Passport** — digital profile: skills, training, certificates, experience, portfolio, endorsements, internship history
- **Portfolio** — work evidence for practical trades (fashion, furniture, welding, electrical, photo/video, catering, carpentry, plumbing, etc.)
- **Internship & mentorship marketplace** — internships, apprenticeships, mentorship, volunteer / short-term placements
- **Jobs marketplace** — trade-categorised jobs, apply with Skills Passport
- **Employer tools** — business profiles, post vacancies, search by skill / location / experience / certification, manage applications
- **Provider tools** — profiles, verification, publish programmes, manage enrolments, view feedback
- **NGO / Government tools** — publish sponsored programmes, define eligibility / places / deadlines. Future: track Selection → Training → Completion → Certification → Placement
- **Search, location-based discovery, notifications, in-platform messaging**
- **Admin portal & dashboard** — users, marketplace, trust & safety, certification, feature flags (Guided Discovery, Reviews, Jobs, Internships, registrations on/off), platform metrics

## Product Principles

1. **Trust First** — verify providers, surface verification + reviews + certification info
2. **Access** — free opportunities remain discoverable alongside paid
3. **Outcomes Over Activity** — measure skills, certification, employment, not just registrations
4. **Simplicity** — find suitable training without specialist knowledge
5. **Opportunity After Training** — turn skills into economic opportunities

## MVP Priorities

1. **Discover:** registration, categories, search, browse, filters, Guided Discovery, recommendations
2. **Trust:** provider registration, verification, badge, listings, reviews, reporting
3. **Training:** programme details, enrolment, dashboard, progress, completion
4. **Certification:** info, pathways, status, Skills Passport
5. **Work:** internships, jobs, employer profiles, learner profiles, portfolio, applications
